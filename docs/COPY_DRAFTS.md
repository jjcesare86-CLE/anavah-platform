**Financial wellness, second level.** "Financial wellness is the stability, and the stress, that comes from how we earn, spend and plan. Money worries are among the most common contributors to anxiety and poor sleep, and they can make it harder to start or stay in care. Anavah talks about financial stress openly as part of treatment planning, helps you understand your options (private pay, insurance, and superbills for out-of-network reimbursement), and explains fees on your consultation call so there are no surprises."
