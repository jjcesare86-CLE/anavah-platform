Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Last updated: October 2026

Anavah Institute wants everyone to be able to use this website. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA, and we are working toward that goal.

What we do

  • Every page can be used with a keyboard, and has a "Skip to content" link and visible focus outlines.
  • Images carry text descriptions, and form fields have labels.
  • Animation, including the moving clouds on the home page, is reduced or turned off if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Pages work on phones, tablets and computers, and text can be enlarged in your browser.

Known limits

Some content is provided by other services: the Google Calendar scheduling page, the map on the home page, Headway, and the Skool community. These have their own accessibility features, and we do not control them. The Mia chat and voice assistant is optional; you can always reach us by phone or email instead.

Tell us about a problem
