Notice of Privacy Practices

Interim version effective October 22, 2026

This notice describes how medical information about you may be used and disclosed and how you can get access to this information. Please review it carefully.

Our commitment to your privacy

Anavah Institute is committed to protecting the privacy of your protected health information (PHI). We are required by law to maintain the privacy of your PHI, to provide you with this notice, and to follow the terms of this notice.

How we may use and disclose your health information

  • Treatment: We may use and disclose your PHI to provide, coordinate, or manage your healthcare and related services, including sharing information with other treating providers with your consent.
  • Payment: We may use and disclose PHI so that treatment and services you receive may be billed to your insurance and payment may be collected.
  • Healthcare operations: We may use PHI for quality assessment, training of clinical personnel, and other business functions.
  • As required by law: We will disclose PHI when required by federal, state, or local law.

Other uses and disclosures

We will not use or disclose your PHI for marketing or sell your PHI without your written authorization. Records of psychotherapy notes are kept separately and are disclosed only as the law allows or with your written authorization. You may revoke an authorization in writing at any time, except where we have already acted on it. In an emergency, or where the law permits or requires it (for example, to prevent a serious threat to health or safety, or for public health reporting), we may disclose the minimum necessary information.

Your rights regarding your PHI

You have the right to:

  • Request restrictions on certain uses of your health information
  • Receive a copy of this notice
  • Inspect and copy your health record
  • Request an amendment to your health record
  • Receive an accounting of disclosures
  • Request confidential communications
  • File a complaint if you believe your privacy rights have been violated

Changes to this notice

We may change this notice and the new terms will apply to all PHI we hold. The current notice is available from the office on request and will be posted here once final.

Our duties

Anavah Institute is required by law to maintain the privacy of your PHI, to provide you with this notice, and to abide by the terms of the notice currently in effect.
