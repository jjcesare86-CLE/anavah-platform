How it works

Four steps, no referral needed.

  1. Complete a brief intake inquiryNo referral needed.
  2. Get matchedTo the right services for your needs.
  3. Meet your care teamYour care coordination and clinical team.
  4. Receive connected, ongoing careWith all providers aligned.

Your first step is an initial consultation call, scheduled on our Google Calendar page. Please share only your name, email and phone when you schedule. Do not include symptoms, diagnoses or medications.

Services

Psychiatric services and coordination.

Core clinical

  • Comprehensive psychiatric evaluations
  • Medication management and optimization
  • Diagnostic clarification, including complex or previously misdiagnosed cases
  • Second opinions on psychiatric treatment plans

Therapeutic approaches

  • Psychotherapy (CBT, ACT, supportive therapy)
  • Trauma-informed care
  • ADHD coaching and executive-functioning strategies
  • Anxiety, depression, and mood disorder management

Specialty and integrative

  • Treatment-resistant depression strategies
  • Psychopharmacology tapering and deprescribing
  • Lifestyle psychiatry: sleep, exercise, nutrition
  • Education and discussion with a prescriber about supplements, the gut–brain axis, and hormonal and metabolic contributors to mental health
  • Bibliotherapy: publishing your memoir

Care coordination

  • Coordination with physical therapists, PCPs and specialists
  • Medical clearance for exercise or rehab programs
  • Disability and functional-capacity documentation
  • Coordination with personal injury attorneys

Information on this site about peptides, GLP-1 medicines, supplements or medications is educational only. Any treatment decision is made with a licensed prescriber.

Billing

Private pay and insurance options.

We offer private pay and insurance options. We do accept certain insurance plans; please contact us directly for details. If we are not in-network, we can help you send superbills to your insurance provider for reimbursement.

Fees, including a sliding fee schedule, are discussed on your consultation call. If you are uninsured or not using insurance, you have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate of expected charges before care begins (cms.gov/nosurprises).

Insurance-billed visits are scheduled by Phillip in Headway after your consultation. View Phillip on Headway.

Medical and legal documentation services must occur during a session.
