Why partner

Six reasons to work with Anavah.

Cross-disciplinary referral network

Connect with vetted mental health clinicians, PTs, OTs, and physiatrists. Give and receive coordinated referrals with clinical context — not cold handoffs.

Shared clinical protocols

Access evidence-based, cross-disciplinary care frameworks — intake tools, communication templates, and treatment alignment protocols — ready to implement.

Training and Lunch and Learns

Training and Lunch and Learns in integrative care coordination, with more courses on the way. See Courses.

Clinic consulting & implementation

We help clinics build integrative care capacity — from workflow redesign to staff training to protocol development — with hands-on consulting support.

B2B subscriptions

Subscription plans for individual providers, small practices, and health systems — with increasing access to tools, training, and coordination infrastructure. Details are shared on a consultation call.

Clinician community

Join a professional network of cross-disciplinary providers who share cases, questions, and insights — a community for the kind of care you want to deliver. Our community on Skool is the place to start. Please do not share identifying patient details.

Who joins

Built for every discipline in the integrative care ecosystem.

Anavah's provider network welcomes clinicians across the integrative care ecosystem: psychiatric and mental health clinicians, primary care, physical and occupational therapists, physiatrists, nutrition and exercise professionals, practice managers, and organizations that want coordinated care for their people.
