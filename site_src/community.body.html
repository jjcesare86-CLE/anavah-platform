Community rules

Please read before you join.

This is a peer community for connection and education. It is not medical care and not for emergencies. In an emergency call 911; for crisis support call or text 988. Please do not share diagnoses, medications, or identifying health details. Be kind. Posts that break these rules will be removed.

Skool is a separate service with its own privacy policy. It is not where your clinical record is kept. Clinical care is limited to patients in Ohio and New York; the community is open worldwide.
