Our Mission

Mission

Anavah is building the infrastructure that coordinates all eight dimensions of wellness into a unified, outcomes-driven ecosystem.

  • EmotionalCoping effectively with life and creating satisfying relationships
  • EnvironmentalGood health by occupying pleasant, stimulating environments that support well-being
  • FinancialSatisfaction with current and future financial situations
  • IntellectualRecognizing creative abilities and finding ways to expand knowledge and skills
  • SocialDeveloping a sense of connection, belonging, and a well-developed support system
  • PhysicalRecognizing the need for physical activity, diet, sleep and nutrition
  • SpiritualExpanding our sense of purpose and meaning in life
  • OccupationalPersonal satisfaction and enrichment derived from one's work

Services

Connecting clinicians. Providing treatment. Improving outcomes.

Connecting Clinicians

Anavah Institute is an integrative healthcare organization designed to bridge the gap between healthcare specialists. We help patients navigate care between clinicians by facilitating coordinated, integrative treatments. We provide education, consultation, and pre-screening to reduce treatment delays. We also offer training, education, to bring new, evidenced based treatment to your clinic.

Providing Integrative Treatment

Anavah Institute provides a range of health services, including psychiatric services, exercise screening, nutritional consultations, integrative blood work, wellness coaching, and much, much more.
