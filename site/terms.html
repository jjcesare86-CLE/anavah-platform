Using this website

This website is provided by Anavah Institute for general information and to help you schedule appointments and contact us. By using it you agree to these terms.

Not medical advice; emergencies

Nothing on this website, including the Mia chat and voice assistant, is medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it does not create a clinician–patient relationship. If you are in an emergency, call 911. For crisis support, call or text 988.

Appointments

Initial consultations are scheduled through our Google Calendar page and are subject to confirmation by the practice. Fees are discussed on the consultation call. Please contact the office about any late-cancellation or missed-appointment policy that applies to your visit. Insurance participation varies by plan; contact us before your visit to confirm coverage.

Good Faith Estimate

If you are uninsured or not using insurance, you have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate of expected charges before you receive care. Ask us when you schedule or at any time. For more information visit cms.gov/nosurprises.
