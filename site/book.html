Appointments

Schedule an initial consultation

Patients and healthcare providers both begin with a consultation call. Choose a time that works for you on our Google Calendar page. If insurance-billed visits are the right next step, Phillip will schedule them with you after your consultation.

Before you book

  • Clinical care is limited to Ohio and New York. Provider services (education, consulting, networking) are available worldwide.
  • Please share only your name, email and phone on the scheduling page. Do not include symptoms, diagnoses or medications.
  • The initial consultation is free for now. Fees for ongoing care are discussed on that call.
  • Good Faith Estimate. If you are uninsured or choose not to use insurance, you have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate of expected charges before care begins. Ask us at scheduling or any time. More information: cms.gov/nosurprises.
